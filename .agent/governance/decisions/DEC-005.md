# DEC-005: Lean reporting and shared-file ownership

## Record Metadata

- **Decision ID**: `DEC-005`

- **Owner**: `trong-hung`

## Context

Three reports per task (~120 files in 10 days) costs too many tokens; shared execution files cause merge conflicts with two people.

## Decision Drivers

- Token budget
- Merge-conflict avoidance

## Options Considered

| Option | Benefits | Costs and Risks | Constraint Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lean` | Implementation + review per task, QA per epic; row-level ownership | Less granular QA | `meets` |
| `full-kit` | Maximum evidence | Token-heavy | `partially-meets` |

## Decision Outcome

- **Selected Option**: `lean`
- **Rationale**: QA reports at `.agent/reports/qa/E-xxx.md`; each PR edits only its own task row; `sessions-history.md` append-only; coordinator trong-hung owns `current-context.md`. Product owner answers in planning session 2026-10-24.
- **Effective Date**: `2026-10-24`

## Consequences

- **Positive**: Fewer files and conflicts.
- **Negative**: QA evidence is per epic, not per task.
- **Follow-up Work**: none

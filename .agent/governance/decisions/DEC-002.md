## Context

The kit defines two task status sets: AGENT.md/task-board (`pending | in-progress | blocked | review | done | cancelled`) and the prompt pack (`BACKLOG -> READY -> IN_PROGRESS -> IMPLEMENTED -> REVIEWING -> APPROVED -> DONE`). The validator enforces the AGENT.md set.

## Decision Drivers

- Validator compatibility
- Single source of truth

## Options Considered

| Option | Benefits | Costs and Risks | Constraint Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| `agent-md-set` | Validator passes; AGENT.md is authoritative | Prompt pack wording differs | `meets` |
| `prompt-pack-set` | More granular | Fails validator | `does-not-meet` |

## Decision Outcome

- **Selected Option**: `agent-md-set`
- **Rationale**: AGENT.md is the authoritative policy. Mapping: IMPLEMENTED/REVIEWING -> `review`, APPROVED -> `done` after review approval. Product owner answers in planning session 2026-10-24.
- **Effective Date**: `2026-10-24`

## Consequences

- **Positive**: Validator-compatible board.
- **Negative**: Prompt-pack status names must be translated mentally.
- **Follow-up Work**: `scripts/agent` enforces the set.
